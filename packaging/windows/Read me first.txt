HashWatch for Windows · Powered by Bitminor
===========================================

1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it (for example Documents\HashWatch).
   Don't run it from inside the zip.

2. Double-click HashWatch.exe.
   Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" because HashWatch
   isn't code-signed yet. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". You only do
   this once.

3. A small console window opens (leave it open; closing it stops HashWatch)
   and your web browser opens the dashboard.

4. Pick one:
   - Connect to my Pi: shows the HashWatch that runs on your Raspberry Pi.
     It finds the Pi at hashwatch.local, or over Tailscale if the Tailscale
     app is installed and signed in. Only the Pi reads your miner.
   - Run here on this PC: this PC reads your miner itself.

Opening HashWatch.exe again just opens your browser to the copy that's
already running (or straight to your Pi).

To change the Pi address or switch to running here, open the
"/?view=pi" link printed in the console window.
