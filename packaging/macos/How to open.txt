How to open Hashwatch on a Mac
==============================

1. Unzip this file (double-click it in Downloads). (Or use Hashwatch.dmg: open it and drag Hashwatch onto Applications.)
2. Drag Hashwatch.app into your Applications folder.
3. Double-click Hashwatch in Applications. Your browser opens the dashboard
   (http://localhost:8787). There is no window or Dock icon: Hashwatch runs
   quietly in the background.

If macOS says Hashwatch "cannot be opened" or "Apple could not verify" it:
   - Click Done (or OK).
   - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   - Scroll down to the Security section and click "Open Anyway" next to Hashwatch.
   - Confirm with Open Anyway and your password or Touch ID.
   You only need to do this once. (Hashwatch is not notarized by Apple yet.)

If macOS asks whether Hashwatch may find devices on your local network,
click Allow. Hashwatch needs that to read your miner.

To open the dashboard again later, double-click Hashwatch again.
To stop Hashwatch, open Settings in the dashboard and click "Quit Hashwatch".
